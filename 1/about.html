About The Firm

Counselors, not just lawyers.

A deliberately small, senior-only firm focused on the kind of personal attention that large firms can't provide and small firms often don't deliver.

Who We Are

Daniel B. Faizakoff P.C. is a premier boutique law firm serving families, entrepreneurs, and closely-held businesses in New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. Founded in 2003, we have spent over two decades building a practice focused on one thing: being the kind of thoughtful, responsive legal advisor our clients wish every lawyer would be.

We are not trying to be everything to everyone. We have chosen three disciplines: estate planning, estate administration & probate, and small business & corporate law. We concentrate on doing those things at a very high level. We decline matters outside our core practice and refer them to lawyers we trust.

How We Work

Our philosophy is simple. Clients retain us because they want a thoughtful advisor a single phone call away: someone who knows their business, understands their family, and treats their matter with the urgency it deserves.

Four commitments shape how we work:

  • Personal attention. Senior counsel on every matter. No handoffs to associates you've never met.
  • Judicious efficiency. We resolve matters economically, often without setting foot in a courtroom.
  • Clear communication. You'll always know where your matter stands and why it stands there.
  • Modern technology. We use the best legal tools available to deliver faster, more cost-efficient work.

The People

Our team is deliberately small and deeply experienced. Between them, our attorneys bring over sixty combined years of practice spanning corporate, estate, tax, securities, and transactional law. They have worked at the SEC, defended Fortune 500 corporations, represented major real estate developers, and counseled clients through some of the most consequential moments in their lives.

Every attorney on this page is someone you can expect to work with directly. That is not a marketing promise. It is how we run the firm.

Recognition

Our firm has been recognized by Super Lawyers and rated AV Preeminent by Martindale-Hubbell year over year for years, the highest peer-review rating available in the legal industry. We are grateful for the recognition, but what matters more to us is the trust our clients place in us year after year, often across multiple generations of a family, or across every stage of a growing business.

Our Offices

We maintain offices in midtown Manhattan and Stamford, Connecticut, serving clients throughout the tri-state region. Many of our clients prefer to meet virtually; we are happy to work however is easiest for you.
