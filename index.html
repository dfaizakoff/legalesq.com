Why Choose Us

Why families and founders choose us.

Most clients arrive carrying something heavy: a parent who just passed, a child being born, a business they have spent a decade building. Legal work in those moments is not just about documents. It is about being heard. We have built our entire practice around that idea.

We are unusually small on purpose. Three senior attorneys, no junior associates, no billable-hour pressure to over-engineer your matter. You will speak with the same lawyer at intake, at signing, and a decade from now.

We explain what we are recommending, why it is the right choice for your circumstances, and how every clause of every document protects you. By the time you sign, you understand your plan as well as we do.

That is what we mean by counselor, not just lawyer: a learned advisor who educates, empowers, and stays with you across the chapters of your life.

How We Work

Counselors, not just lawyers.

Clients retain us for more than legal work. They retain us because they want a thoughtful advisor a single phone call away: someone who knows their business, understands their family, and treats their matter with the urgency it deserves.

01

Emotional intelligence on every call

Estate planning surfaces marriages, mortality, money, and difficult family stories. We treat that material with patience, discretion, and zero judgment.

02

Education before execution

We explain everything in plain English. You will leave every meeting understanding what we are doing, why we are doing it, and how it serves you. An informed client makes better decisions, and stays a client for life.

03

One firm, one phone number, for decades

We have clients in their third decade with us. That continuity is rare in law and impossible to fake.

04

Flat-fee transparency

Basic estate planning for a flat fee. You can call as often as you need without watching a meter run. No client should ever skip a question because it costs $500 to ask.
